    Groups

    Groups let one project hold multiple circuit diagrams. Pick a group as the source via Src on a pinned Circuit — schematic, build list, and analysis then use that group’s assets and wires (circuit engine, materials bridges unchanged).

    Letter tabs are subgroups (a, b, c…) for organisation and iterations. Click to select (Shift-click to add). Double-click a letter to enable/disable that subgroup — disabled parts stay in the project but are dimmed and left out of circuit Src. Absorbing another group via + keeps that group’s name and colour as a subgroup; removing those members restores the original group.

    Drawn wires can be grouped for workspace organisation (select / move / subgroups). Circuit Src schematic and analysis follow asset connectivity and enabled subgroups — not wire membership tags.

    Rename the group. Each top-level group gets its own selection-frame colour (yellow, pink, orange, green…). Auto names stay Group 1, Group 2… by order; consolidating prunes emptied numbers and redirects circuit Src to the receiving group.

    + Enter add mode (selection clears). Click another group to select it for consume (Shift-click to add more groups), or pick loose assets and wires. Press Enter or + again — consumed groups become lettered subgroups and keep their name and colour. Esc cancels.

    − Enter remove mode (selection clears). Select members or wires inside the group — or click a subgroup tab to select that whole subgroup — with normal selection visuals. Press Enter or − again to peel them out. Esc cancels.

    Tip: a normal click on a grouped asset selects the whole group (assets + grouped wires); double-click or marquee deep-selects individuals without moving the whole group.
